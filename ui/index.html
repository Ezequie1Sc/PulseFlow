Camera & privacy

SmartSpectra uses your camera to estimate pulse and breathing rate on this device. Your Presage key is used for online authentication.

Presage normally collects session summaries and device diagnostics. PulseFlow disables SDK telemetry. Its preview, history and summary stay in memory. Clear session data or close the app to discard them.
